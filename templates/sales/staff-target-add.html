How Targets Work

Monthly Reset

Each month has its own target. Performance is automatically compared at month-end.

Auto-Tracked

Activity logging (calls, meetings, proposals) updates counters in real-time.

Ratings

Staff are rated as Top Performer, On Track, Needs Push, or Needs Review based on combined achievement.

Required Fields
  • Staff Member — who this target is for
  • Month & Year — the performance period
  • At least one activity target value
  • A target per staff per month is unique — editing updates it
